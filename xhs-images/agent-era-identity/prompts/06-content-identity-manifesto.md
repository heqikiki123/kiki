A minimalist manifesto poster for Xiaohongshu, screen-print aesthetic, purely typographic.

**Canvas**: Portrait 3:4, 1080×1440px
**Color palette**: Pure black background, white text fading from grey (top) to bright white (bottom), electric blue (#00BFFF) for the final punchline. Subtle halftone texture at ~6% opacity.

**Layout** (sparse — poem-like stacked lines, wide line spacing):
- Small grey label at top: 我为什么叫"硅基接口人"
- Thin divider
- Body lines (each on its own line, text gradually brightens toward bottom):
  - (grey) 不是因为我懂更多AI工具
  - (grey) 而是因为我在思考——
  - (medium white) 当AI可以自主行动时
  - (white) 人类站在哪里？
  - (white) 做什么？
  - (white bold) 成为什么？
- Spacer
- Punchline (electric blue, bold, larger): 这个问题的答案
- Sub-punchline (electric blue, slightly smaller): 比任何Prompt技巧都重要
- Bottom-right small grey: @未来硅基接口人

**Typography**: Lines left-aligned, generous vertical spacing, like a poem or speech transcript.
**Mood**: Dark, philosophical, like a message broadcast from deep space. Sparse and powerful.
